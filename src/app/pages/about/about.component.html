About Cultivator

Cultivator is a budgeting app that allows you to work with transactions stored inside Google Sheets. With Cultivator, you can update the transaction category or add notes on the go, without the need to open Google Sheets.

Cultivator works well with the popular personal finance tool, Tiller. It is able to read from your Google Sheet the transactions and categories created by the Tiller template, then write back the updates you make from the app.
